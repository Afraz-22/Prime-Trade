About GLOBAL PARTNERS

GLOBAL PARTNERS is a diversified sourcing, trading, and supply company based in Bangladesh. We serve businesses across different industries by providing garment-related materials, poultry products, and machinery sourcing services tailored to customer requirements.

Our business is built around understanding customer needs, identifying suitable products, coordinating with suppliers, and offering practical sourcing solutions at competitive prices.

With a flexible approach to product sourcing and supply, we aim to simplify purchasing for our customers while maintaining a strong focus on product suitability, quality, and dependable service.

Our Products & Services

GLOBAL PARTNERS offers a diverse range of products and sourcing services to meet the requirements of customers across the garment, poultry, and industrial sectors.

A. Garment Materials & Printing Supplies

We supply garment-related materials and production consumables for the garment, textile, printing, and customization industries.

Our product range includes:

• Screen-printing chemicals
• Hot-melt adhesive powders
• Heat-transfer films
• Different types of vinyl materials
• Garment accessories and production supplies
• Decorative stones and stone-related materials
• Stone paper used in relevant garment applications
• Other garment-related materials according to customer requirements

We aim to help customers source suitable materials for their production processes with competitive pricing and reliable supply.

B. Poultry Products & Farm Supplies

We trade and supply a range of poultry-related products and farm equipment to meet the needs of poultry farms, dealers, and businesses.

Our product range includes:

• Poultry plastic products
• Poultry feed
• Poultry feed tanks and storage solutions
• Cooling pads for poultry farms
• Glass wool and related insulation materials
• Poultry-farming equipment and accessories
• Other poultry-related products based on customer requirements

We focus on sourcing practical, cost-effective products to support poultry-farming operations.

C. Machinery Import & Sourcing

We provide customized machinery sourcing services based on customer specifications and project requirements.

Our sourcing capabilities include:

• Garment and textile machinery
• Printing and heat-transfer machinery
• Industrial machinery and equipment
• Machinery components and spare parts
• Other machinery as requested by customers

We work to identify suitable suppliers and products based on technical specifications, budget, intended application, and delivery requirements.

D. Customized Product Sourcing

Beyond our core product categories, we assist customers in sourcing a wide range of commercially viable products according to their specific needs.

Whether you require regular supplies, specialized materials, machinery, or a product not listed in our catalogue, GLOBAL PARTNERS is ready to discuss your requirements and explore suitable sourcing options.
